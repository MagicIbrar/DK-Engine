BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Where do I play?

Play Now opens the game on the host you are currently visiting. The host needs the engine and original client data. See Downloads for the local launcher and setup guide.

Are my website and game accounts the same?

Yes. Create your ID here, then use it at the game's login screen. Your account page displays characters saved on this host.

Where is my progress stored?

Account progress is saved by this private host. Register inside the launcher or on this website, then sign into the game. Blank credentials do not open guest play. An existing signed-in session is restored automatically. My Account offers backups and optional archives of older local characters.

What is available in the shared world?

The server verifies movement, combat, supported skills and item transactions, ordinary quests and reward boxes, mounts and seven-member parties. Guilds, trading, instances, PvP, pets and advanced services still have gaps. Imported local characters remain sandbox characters.

What appears in the rankings?

The rankings list server-verified characters with a positive rebirth count from this host. Imported sandbox characters are excluded. If nobody has rebirthed yet, the board stays empty.
