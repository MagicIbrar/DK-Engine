Host a world

Choose Open or Invite only in the launcher Host panel. Online relay hosting gives players an HTTPS address without publishing your home IP. Only open worlds that explicitly opt in can join the public directory. Permanent hosting requires a configured relay and directory; the temporary test mode is not a production service.

INSTALLED LAUNCHER

Host from your computer

Open Client.exe in your installation folder, then choose the Host panel. Choose Open or Invite only and configure the relay before sharing its HTTPS address. After signing in there, choose Launch client or Launch browser. This page cannot start the launcher or configure your computer automatically.
